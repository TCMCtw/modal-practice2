調式練習二MODAL DRILL 2 · SING-ALONG

開口唱練習 · 七個調式,同一個起始音,上行下行交替 · Seven modes, one starting note, up and down

這是什麼?WHAT IS THIS

在鋼琴上只彈白鍵,從不同的白鍵開始各彈七個音,就得到七種調式:從 C 開始是 Ionian(大調),從 D 開始是 Dorian,從 E 開始是 Phrygian,從 F 開始是 Lydian,從 G 開始是 Mixolydian,從 A 開始是 Aeolian(自然小調),從 B 開始是 Locrian。

這個練習把七種調式移到同一個起始音上,上行、下行交替各唱一次:大調 Ionian、弗里吉安 Phrygian、米索利底安 Mixolydian、洛克里安 Locrian 上行,多里安 Dorian、利底安 Lydian、自然小調 Aeolian 下行。唱名用「首調」:照每個調式背後的大調(母調)來唱,所以同一個起始音,七種調式會從七個不同的唱名開始。

Play only the white keys on a piano, seven notes at a time, starting from a different white key each time, and you get the seven modes: from C comes Ionian (the major scale), from D Dorian, from E Phrygian, from F Lydian, from G Mixolydian, from A Aeolian (the natural minor), from B Locrian. This drill moves all seven modes onto the same starting note and sings them alternating up and down: Ionian, Phrygian, Mixolydian and Locrian ascend; Dorian, Lydian and Aeolian descend. Sing in movable do, using the solfege of each mode's parent major scale, so the same starting note begins on seven different syllables.

怎麼練HOW TO PRACTISE

先聽一遍,第二遍開始跟著唱。唱名的「do」永遠是母調的主音:例如 Dorian 從「re」開始,因為它是母調的第二音。下行的調式從高八度的同一個音往下唱。七種調式都唱完,換一個起始音再來。

Listen once, then sing along. "Do" is always the tonic of the parent major scale: Dorian starts on "re" because it begins on the second note of its parent scale. Descending modes start from the same note one octave higher and sing down. After all seven modes, pick a new starting note and go again.
